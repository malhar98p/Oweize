FAQ's

Everything you need to know about Oweize.

Is Oweize free to use?
Yes. Oweize is completely free to download and use on iOS, Android, and the web. There are no hidden fees or premium tiers for core features.
How is it different from Splitwise?
Oweize is built for speed and simplicity. It offers receipt scanning, real-time balance syncing across all devices, and a clean interface that gets out of your way.
Does it support multiple currencies?
Yes. You can log expenses in any of 158 supported currencies. Balances are tracked in the currency they were recorded in and settlements happen in the same currency.
Is my data safe?
Oweize only stores your expense data. We do not collect any bank or payment information.
